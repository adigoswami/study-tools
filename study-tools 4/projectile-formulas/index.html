Conventions used everywhere on this page

  • g = 9.81 m/s², air resistance ignored.
  • Case A (horizontal launch from a height): down is positive for vertical quantities, so the vertical acceleration is +g.
  • Cases B and C (launch at an angle): up is positive, so the vertical acceleration is −g. In Case C the origin is the ground under the launch point.
  • The calculator keeps full precision between steps; numbers shown are rounded.
