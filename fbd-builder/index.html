Rules for every free-body diagram
  • Weight (mg) always points straight down. Everything near Earth has it.
  • Normal force (N) comes from a surface the object touches. It is perpendicular to the surface, pushing away.
  • Tension (T) comes from a rope or string. It pulls along the rope, away from the object.
  • Friction (f) comes from a rough surface. It is parallel to the surface and opposes sliding (or the tendency to slide).
  • Air resistance (D) opposes the velocity. Applied force (F) is a push or pull by a person or thing.
  • No agent means no force. There is no "force of motion". A moving object keeps moving by inertia (First Law).
