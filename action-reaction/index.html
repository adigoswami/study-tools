Action–Reaction Pair Finder

Newton's Third Law practice: find each force's partner, and spot the forces that only look like pairs.

The recipe (Hewitt §5.3): write the force as "A exerts a force on B". Its reaction is "B exerts a force on A": same size, opposite direction, same type, acting on the other object.

    Follows Physics Session 6 (Hewitt Ch5 §5.1–5.7; Glencoe Ch4 §4.3).
